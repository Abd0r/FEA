[SCENARIO 7] what M17 deliberately does not claim
  recovery at H = 10 ms       : 30.001000 ms against tau 52.254329 ms

  none of the following is established here:
  - no rescue port exists, so nothing above has ever been exercised on
    hardware; the mechanism is specified, not demonstrated.
  - H and m are declared. Neither is measured, and FZC-v0 lists both as
    open parameters.
  - the Slingshot latency is unsourced; scenario 3 exists to show it does
    not carry the result rather than to endorse a value for it.
  - congestion rates are SWEPT. No chip traffic has produced one.
  - scenario 6 is a rule demonstration, not a timed partition, so it shows
    the fence rejects stale epochs, not how fast it does so.
  - heartbeat bandwidth across every Zone IS reported, in scenario 5,
    but from a declared packet size rather than from a real packet format.
  label: capability PROPOSED, latency OPEN, mechanism NOT DEMONSTRATED.
  NEXT EVIDENCE GATE: a physical rescue port, and a timed partition with
  measured congestion from a real Slingshot load.
