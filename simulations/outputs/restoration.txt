[SCENARIO 1] a passive path across the die collapses without restoration
  die edge                    : 0.707 cm (7071.068 um)
  declared decay length       : 1.000 um
  end-of-chain amplitude      : 10^-3070.9
  next-stage input threshold  : 0.1 (10^-1.0)
  passes threshold?           : NO
  shortfall                   : 3069.9 orders of magnitude
  finding: Reviewer 4 #7 is correct. Without gain the signal is gone long
  before it crosses the die. This is not a margin problem, it is a 3070-order collapse.

[SCENARIO 2] restoration endpoints required to cross the die
  max spacing at threshold    : 2.302585 um
  endpoints for one die edge  : 3070
  amplitude after one hop     : 0.100000  (threshold 0.100000, equal by construction)
  so restoration is mandatory, and it needs roughly 3070 endpoints per edge.
  label: decay length and threshold are declared. The structural result is
  that restoration is required at all, and its density follows from decay.

[SCENARIO 3] every restoration endpoint costs area and power
  endpoints per edge          : 3070

[SCENARIO 5] restoration needs an energy source, not the signal itself
  endpoints                  : 9431041
  power from a bias/clock rail: 0.009431 W
  a passive DBW cannot amplify. Every endpoint needs supplied energy.
  this is the same constraint FZC's actuator gate already states: a stored
  pattern is not gain, and a decaying signal is not a restoration source.
  the rail that feeds these endpoints must appear in M1 as boundary or
  regional power, and its routing must appear in M2 as area.
