[SCENARIO 5] what M18 deliberately does not claim
  routing budget under test  : 0.250000 cm^2

  none of the following is established here:
  - no power figure is produced. Rescue activity has no model, so this
    module adds nothing to M1's budget and the four declared terms stay
    unsourced.
  - wire pitch and per-transistor area are both DECLARED. There is no
    public 2 nm PDK, so every threshold here is swept, not sourced.
  - how a stateless port Block tells a rescue tag from ordinary traffic
    without retained state is UNVALIDATED DEVICE PHYSICS. This module
    shows where such a receiver could be PAID FOR, not that it works.
  - L1 still depends on the neighbour link being alive. The peer set,
    quorum and L2/L3 ladder cover that, but no tier was timed here.
  label: area ARITHMETIC and SWEPT, mechanism NOT DEMONSTRATED.
  NEXT EVIDENCE GATE: a device model for stateless tag detection, and a
  power estimate for rescue activity before it enters M1.
