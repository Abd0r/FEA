[SCENARIO 1] ideal absorption and simulated single-pass capture disagree
  ideal A(E_F)              : 1.000
  wavepacket single pass    : 0.460
  unexplained gap           : 0.540 (217.4% of ideal)
  V2 attributed this to unspecified finite-pulse effects with no mechanism.
  label: both values are V2's own. The gap stays open until a finite-pulse
  calculation derives 0.46 from the same Hamiltonian that gives A(E_F) = 1.

[SCENARIO 2] independent-Bernoulli redundancy reproduces V2's 18-fire figure
  P_abs = 0.460000, target 0.999900
  minimum fires for target     : 15
  success at 18 fires          : 0.999985
  success at 1 fire            : 0.460000
  V2's 18 fires reach 1.0000, which covers its 99.99% claim.
  but 15 fires already suffice, so 18 is conservative or came from a different target.
  either way the whole result rests on the independence assumption.

[SCENARIO 3] a common-mode block fraction puts a hard ceiling on fidelity
  common-mode fraction 0.00  ->  ceiling 1.0000   99.99% reachable in 15 fires
  common-mode fraction 0.01  ->  ceiling 0.9900   99.99% UNREACHABLE at any fire count
  common-mode fraction 0.02  ->  ceiling 0.9800   99.99% UNREACHABLE at any fire count
  common-mode fraction 0.05  ->  ceiling 0.9500   99.99% UNREACHABLE at any fire count
  common-mode fraction 0.10  ->  ceiling 0.9000   99.99% UNREACHABLE at any fire count
  a 5% shared failure fraction caps fidelity at 95.0%, and no number of retries crosses it.
  V2's 99.99% claim is therefore conditional on exactly zero correlation,
  which has not been measured for the 5-atom cluster.

[SCENARIO 4] program pass rate must follow from the capture model
  V2 program pass: 96% vector-add, 98% dot-product, both 16 elements
  implied per-op success for 96% over 16 ops : 0.99745
  implied per-op success for 98% over 16 ops : 0.99874
  model per-op success at 18 fires, independent : 0.99998
  model per-op success at 18 fires, 2% correlated: 0.97999
  program pass at 16 ops, independent            : 0.9998
  program pass at 16 ops, 2% correlated           : 0.7236
  finding: V2's reported 96-98% program rates are far WORSE than its own
  18-fire independent model predicts, yet the paper never links the two.
  either the program runs use fewer fires than 18, or another error source
  exists that the capture model does not contain:
  program success and SECDED are asserted, not derived from one model.
