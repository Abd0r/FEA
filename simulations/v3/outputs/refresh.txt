Energy per rewritten bit is declared, not measured. The range is the result.

[SCENARIO 4] tighter retention margin multiplies refresh power
  interval = tau/N   interval(s)    refresh power(W)   basis
  N = 2.0e+00          2.6e-02          7.2e-04   V2 design (line 373)
  N = 3.0e+00          1.7e-02          1.1e-03   conservative
  N = 1.0e+00          5.2e-02          3.6e-04   refresh at tau
  N = 5.0e-01          1.0e-01          1.8e-04   slack
  V2's tau/2 costs 2.0x tau/1; the conservative tau/3 costs 1.5x V2's policy.
  so the divisor, the refresh margin and the energy per bit are all load-bearing
  and all stated. That is why V3 reports a range, not V2's 1.1e-4.

  at V2's design point: local duty 3.6832e-06, refresh power 7.1766e-04 W at the declared energy.
  WITHDRAWN: an earlier revision solved for Ec = 0.82 eV to fit refresh under
  V2's advertised port. That target was an artifact of assuming refresh crosses
  shared routing, which M10 scenario 4 has withdrawn. There is no port constraint.

  so raising Ec is not about feasibility, it is about ENERGY. Each 0.05 eV
  cuts refresh work by roughly exp(0.05/kT) = 6.9x.
  from 0.65 to 0.70 eV, refresh power falls about 7x for 0.05 eV of extra barrier.
  two levers V2 does not discuss: refresh only the active region instead of the
  whole array, or relax tau/2 to tau/1 for 2x less work at 2x the risk.
  none of this repairs a desorbed atom. Refresh restores charge, not structure.
  separate finding: FEA-architecture.pdf lists tau = 362 s at Ec = 0.70 eV while
  its own f_K = 2.76 /s implies 0.362 s. That preprint row has a 1000x slip.
  label: floor and energy derived from V2's Kramers formula. Ec UNMEASURED.

[SCENARIO 6] who refreshes the FZC: the controller maintains itself
  FZC Blocks to maintain        : 535
  grouped into Words            : 9 groups of 64, one group per FIRE
  expected FIRE latency         : 93.1573 ps
  time for one full rotation    : 0.8384 ns
  refresh interval (tau/2)      : 26.1272 ms
  FZC rotation duty             : 3.209e-08
  tau (retention)               : 52.2543 ms
  longest possible staleness    : one rotation, 1.604e-08 of tau
  full pass incl. FZC           : 96.2315 ns
  answer: the FZC SCHEDULES refresh, a local FIRE primitive PERFORMS it,
  and the FZC's own Blocks are part of that same maintenance pass, rotated
  in 9 groups so the controller never vanishes.
  critical state is triple-replicated by the ledger, so no single group
  ever holds the only copy of Zone ID, routing state or the refresh pointer.
  the infinite regress stops because staleness is bounded by a ROTATION,
  not by an interval: 1.604e-08 of tau worst case.
  at power-on, before any FZC state exists, the boundary boot controller
  initialises it; after that the fabric maintains itself locally.
  label: grouping and duty DERIVED from the ledger and M9 latency; the
  schedule-vs-primitive split is PROPOSED; the heartbeat source for a
  cold Zone with no valid FZC is OPEN.
