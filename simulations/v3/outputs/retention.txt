[SCENARIO 3] retention versus charging energy at 300 K
  Ec = 0.3 eV -> tau = 0.0001 ms
  Ec = 0.4000 eV -> tau = 0.0033 ms
  Ec = 0.5000 eV -> tau = 0.1578 ms
  Ec = 0.6500 eV -> tau = 52.2543 ms
  Ec = 0.7000 eV -> tau = 361.4809 ms
  Ec=0.3 eV collapses retention by orders of magnitude. The design point is a cliff,
  not a plateau. V2's single-point claim hides that.

[SCENARIO 4] retention sets the refresh contract the system must pay
  tau = 52.2543 ms -> refresh interval 26.1272 ms -> 38 passes/s
  moving 0e14 bits (data + FZC) every 26.1272 ms is 90440.314 GB/s of refresh traffic alone.
  array size is 2362.9 GB.
  to clear it once per 26.1272 ms the fabric must sustain 90440 GB/s of refresh traffic alone.
  V2 called refresh overhead < 1e-4. That assumed a pass much cheaper than
  clearing 2362.9 GB inside 26.1 ms. This traffic must be charged to M1 power
  and to M9/M10 bandwidth, and it shares Slingshot with real work.

[SCENARIO 5] the Kramers prefactor is an assumption, not a measurement
  nominal nu0 (bulk optical phonon) : 52.25 ms
  nu0 x 10 (faster local mode)      : 5.23 ms
  nu0 / 10 (slower local mode)      : 522.54 ms
  No local-mode measurement exists for the 5-atom cluster, so retention at 300 K
  stays an open experimental question. This module bounds it; it does not close it.
  label: derived formula, estimated inputs, open physical validation.
